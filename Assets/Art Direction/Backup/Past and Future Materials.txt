TIME TRAVEL PROJECTION BACKUP
Past and Future art direction

Shared layout
Use the original hand-drawn city plan as the reference. Buildings
stay on their lots, the main street stays open, and the plaza stays
in the same place. The concept images are captures of the hand-built
ProBuilder models in the Unity Editor.

Past village
Limestone for low walls, Charcoal for pitched roofs, Sage for the
village green and trees, and Slate for the well and buggy body.
Past Earth colors the dirt road and Past Chestnut colors the horse.
Drag cubes over the house footprints and place prisms on top. Use
a shallow cube for the road, cones over cube trunks for the four
trees, and a cylinder for the well. The buggy uses a cube body,
a prism hood, and four duplicated cylinder wheels. Small cubes
make up the horse and the two hitch rails.

Future skyline
Slate for tall towers and lower buildings, Limestone for inset
tower crowns and the beacon, Charcoal for the road, and Sage for the
plaza. Drag cubes over the same lots, set different heights, and add
one cone as the plaza landmark.

The reusable material swatches are under Assets > Materials.
The shared sky and ambience carry through all three timelines.
