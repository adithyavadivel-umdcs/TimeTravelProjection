PRESENT WORLD
Material and surface reference

CONCEPT
The hand-drawn city plan is the concept image. Keep the central street
open, use tall rectangular towers on the marked lots, and make the
plaza a simple green space. Use flat colors so the silhouettes and
window bands read clearly from a distance.

PALETTE
Open Assets > Materials in Unity to see the material swatches.

Present Limestone    Warm beige     Tower walls
Present Slate        Blue-gray      Wraparound windows and car cabins
Present Charcoal     Dark gray      Road, tires, and tree trunk
Present Sage         Muted green    Plaza and tree canopy
New Material         Off-white      Ground surrounding the road

SURFACE TREATMENT
The manual scene uses flat materials. Use thin boxes for the window
bands, a flat box for the road, and a cube and cone for the plaza tree.
